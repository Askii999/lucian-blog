When the city goes dark, the screen stays lit. The fans inside the case keep up a steady hum, like a tide unwilling to stop.

We spend so much time building order, making sure every byte lands at its assigned address, until the errors are gone and the cursor blinks quietly against the bash. But some things cannot be compiled.

The  whisper of leaves in the dawn . A chord played wrong . A narrow gap  between two tall buildings. The unexplained hesitations, the unsent words, the echoes moving slowly through the body—unless we catch them, they slip away into the night.

So this corner came to be.   beside the  unceasing stream of information, taking in unfinished sentences and the background noise that has yet to fade.
